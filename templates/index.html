Disabled
◈
Test mode is the defaultScheduled sends are logged without delivery. To enable real sending, set LIVE_SENDS_ENABLED=true on the server after connecting an authorized, confirmed-consent audience.
Before enabling live sending: verify your sender domain, connect your Brevo list, configure the double opt-in template, publish your privacy notice, and test unsubscribe handling.
